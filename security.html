Security & trust

Your data stays yours.

Firms trust Rubriq with internal documents, evidence, and assessments. Here is exactly how we protect them, in plain language.

Dedicated by design: your own platform, your own database.

Every firm's platform runs on its own separate project, with its own database and file storage. Your assessments, evidence, reports, and knowledge base are never stored alongside another client's data.

Where your data lives

Your platform is hosted on Cloudflare, with your database and files on Supabase. You choose the hosting region when your platform is set up, so your data can stay close to home.

Encrypted everywhere

All traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Your database and uploaded files are encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.

Role-based access

Administrators, consultants, and respondents each see only what their role allows. Accounts use email and password, with two-factor authentication for administrators and consultants. Single sign-on with Microsoft or Google is available on request.

Private evidence files

Uploaded evidence is kept in private storage and is never publicly accessible. Files open through short-lived secure links, only for people with access to that assessment.

Daily backups

Your database is backed up automatically every day, so your data can be restored if something goes wrong.

AI that doesn't learn from you

Reports are written by enterprise AI models (currently Anthropic's Claude) through business API access. Your assessments, evidence, and knowledge base are not used to train AI models, and your knowledge base is only ever used for your own reports.

Humans approve first

Nothing is generated until your team reviews and approves the assessment. Every report is grounded only in approved scores, evidence, and notes.

Export and deletion

Your data is yours. We export everything on request, and permanently delete it when you ask or when our engagement ends.

Confidentiality first

We're happy to sign an NDA before you share any framework or data. Our team accesses your platform only to set it up and support it.

Rubriq is built on infrastructure providers with independent security audits: Cloudflare (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001) and Supabase (SOC 2 Type II). These certifications belong to our providers, not to Rubriq itself.
