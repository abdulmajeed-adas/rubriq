How it works

One framework in, a finished report out.

01

Load your framework

Domains, criteria, weights, and a maturity or compliance scale — modeled once, reused every cycle.

02

Clients self-assess

A guided scoring form per criterion, with evidence upload and space for supporting notes.

03

Consultant reviews

A dashboard to confirm or adjust every score before anything gets written up.

04

AI drafts the report

A full narrative report and Excel action plan — grounded only in submitted data, nothing invented.

What ships with it

Everything past the scoring form.

AI-written narrative reports

Executive summary, per-criterion analysis, root-cause writeups, and strategic recommendations — built only from submitted scores and evidence, never fabricated figures.

Consultant review dashboard

Every submission in one place, scores confirmable or adjustable before generation, past reports saved and re-editable.

Auto-generated action plan

Recommendations translated straight into a real Excel workbook — projects, activities, and a timeline, matching your own template.

Evidence & file management

Supporting documents attached per criterion, stored securely and linked straight into the review flow.

Full assessment history

Every past cycle saved and reopenable, so clients can be tracked over time, not just scored once.
