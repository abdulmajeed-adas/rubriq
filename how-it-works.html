How it works

One framework in, a finished report out.

01

Load your framework

Domains, criteria, weights, and a maturity or scoring scale — modeled once, reused every cycle.

02

Clients self-assess

A guided scoring form per criterion, with evidence uploads and space for supporting notes.

03

Consultant reviews

A dashboard to confirm or adjust every score before anything gets generated.

04

AI generates the report

Polished narrative reports and development plans, generated automatically from approved scores, evidence, and consultant feedback.
